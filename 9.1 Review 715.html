햇볕에 너무 심하게 타서 피부에 물집이 생기는 정도의 화상을 말해요. 보통은 second-degree sunburn처럼 하이픈을 넣어 씁니다.

end up

계획하지 않았던 결과에 결국 도달했을 때 자주 쓰는 표현이에요. 보통 end up + -ing 또는 end up + 장소/상태 형태로 사용합니다.

I grew up in Ohio, and for most of my childhood, I thought I would become a dentist. My father was a dentist, my uncle was a dentist, and everyone seemed to assume I would be one too.

I lasted exactly one semester in dental school.

I hated being indoors all day, so I quit and took a temporary job at a summer camp in California. I was supposed to work there for three months. Instead, I ended up staying on the West Coast for almost seven years.

My first job after the camp was selling surfboards. This was strange because I couldn't surf. The owner eventually decided that his employees should understand the products, so he gave me a free lesson.

That lesson sent me to the hospital.

I stayed in the water for hours without enough sunscreen and got a second-degree sunburn across my shoulders and back. I had blisters everywhere. For several days, I couldn't wear a normal shirt.

You would think that experience would have made me avoid the ocean. It did the opposite. After I recovered, I learned to surf properly.

A few years later, I went to Hawaii for what was supposed to be a ten-day vacation. While I was there, I met a woman who ran boat tours. One of her employees suddenly quit, and she offered me his job.

So I ended up living in Hawaii for two years.

Working on boats made me interested in marine animals, and eventually I went back to university to study marine biology. I was thirty-one by then and much older than most of the other students.

After graduating, I joined a research project in Australia. That was supposed to last six months, but I ended up working there for four years.

Then my life changed direction completely.

My mother became ill, so I moved back to Ohio to help my parents. While I was there, a friend asked me to help manage her small outdoor-equipment store. I knew a lot about camping, surfing, boats, and travel by that point, so it suited me surprisingly well.

Eventually, she retired and sold the business to me.

That's what I do now.

I sometimes laugh when customers ask how I got into this kind of work. The answer is difficult because there wasn't really one decision. I quit dental school, worked at a camp, got badly sunburned, learned to surf, moved to Hawaii, studied fish, lived in Australia, and somehow ended up running an outdoor shop fifteen minutes from the house where I grew up.

Life can be very inefficient.

그 경험을 하고 나면 바다를 피했을 것 같죠. 그런데 정반대였어요. 회복한 뒤에 저는 제대로 서핑을 배웠습니다.

몇 년 뒤, 원래 열흘 동안만 휴가를 보내려고 하와이에 갔어요. 그곳에서 보트 투어 회사를 운영하는 여성을 만났는데, 직원 한 명이 갑자기 그만두면서 저에게 그 자리를 제안했어요.

그래서 저는 결국 하와이에서 2년을 살게 됐습니다.

보트에서 일하면서 해양 동물에 관심이 생겼고, 결국 대학으로 돌아가 해양생물학을 공부했어요. 당시 저는 서른한 살이었고 대부분의 다른 학생들보다 훨씬 나이가 많았죠.

졸업한 뒤에는 호주의 연구 프로젝트에 참여했습니다. 원래 6개월짜리 프로젝트였지만, 저는 결국 그곳에서 4년 동안 일하게 됐어요.

그러다 제 인생의 방향이 완전히 바뀌었습니다.

어머니가 아프셔서 부모님을 돕기 위해 오하이오로 돌아왔어요. 그곳에 있는 동안 친구 한 명이 작은 아웃도어 용품점을 운영하는 걸 도와달라고 했습니다. 그때쯤 저는 캠핑, 서핑, 보트, 여행에 대해 꽤 많이 알고 있었기 때문에 생각보다 잘 맞았어요.

결국 친구는 은퇴했고 그 가게를 저에게 팔았습니다.

지금 제가 하는 일이 바로 그 일이에요.

가끔 손님들이 제가 어떻게 이런 일을 하게 됐냐고 물으면 웃음이 납니다. 대답하기가 어렵거든요. 딱 하나의 결정이 있었던 게 아니니까요. 치과대학을 그만두고, 캠프에서 일하고, 햇볕에 심하게 타고, 서핑을 배우고, 하와이로 가고, 물고기를 공부하고, 호주에서 살다가, 어떻게 된 건지 결국 제가 자란 집에서 차로 15분 거리에 있는 아웃도어 용품점을 운영하게 됐어요.

인생은 참 비효율적일 때가 있습니다.
