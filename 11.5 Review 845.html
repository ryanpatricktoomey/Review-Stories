get something off your chest

마음속에 쌓인 걱정이나 불만을 누군가에게 털어놓아 후련해지는 것을 뜻해요. I need to get something off my chest.처럼 자연스럽게 사용해요.

flashing their high beams

자동차의 상향등을 반복해서 켰다 껐다 하는 행동이에요. 앞차에 신호를 보내거나 비키라고 압박할 때 사용하기도 해요.

ghost

명사로는 '유령'이지만, 동사로는 아무런 설명 없이 갑자기 연락을 끊는다는 뜻이에요. He ghosted me.는 '그 사람이 갑자기 연락을 끊었어요'라는 뜻이에요.

bottled up

화, 슬픔, 스트레스 같은 감정을 표현하지 않고 마음속에 계속 쌓아 두는 것을 뜻해요. bottled-up anger는 '억눌린 분노'라는 뜻이에요.

Okay, I need to get something off my chest. I absolutely cannot stand the way some people drive!

Seriously, what happens to people when they get behind the wheel? Do they suddenly forget that everyone else on the road is a human being?

Yesterday, I was driving home from work, minding my own business, when this enormous SUV appeared behind me.

I was driving at the speed limit. Not slowly. Not dangerously. The actual speed limit!

But apparently, that wasn't good enough for His Majesty in the SUV.

He started flashing his high beams at me.

Once. Twice. Three times!

What exactly did he expect me to do? Break the law because he was in a hurry?

Then he honked.

Oh, I'm sorry! Was driving legally somehow offensive to you?

I tried to avoid looking in my rearview mirror because I could feel myself getting angry.

But he kept getting closer!

I swear, some drivers think the road belongs to them.

And don't get me started on people who change lanes without signaling.

What's so difficult about using your turn signal? It's literally right next to your steering wheel!

You don't even have to get out of your seat!

And then there are the drivers who park wherever they want.

Sidewalks? Sure!

Crosswalks? Why not!

Right in front of someone's driveway? Apparently, that's perfectly acceptable too!

Last month, someone blocked the entrance to our apartment parking lot. I called the number on the windshield five times.

No answer.

I sent a message.

Nothing.

The driver basically ghosted me!

Eventually, I reported them because nobody could get in or out.

And when the driver finally appeared, he was angry at ME!

He said, "You could have just waited."

WAITED?!

For how long? Until you finished your shopping? Until next Christmas?

Look, I usually try to stay calm. I don't enjoy confrontation. I bite my tongue because I know getting angry won't solve anything.

But all that frustration gets bottled up.

And sometimes I just can't take it anymore!

My wife says I should stop complaining about bad drivers.

She tells me, "Just ignore them."

Ignore them? They're driving two-ton vehicles around other people!

That's not something you can simply ignore!

And here's what really drives me crazy. These same people probably think they're excellent drivers!

They probably go home and complain about everyone else on the road!

Maybe I should just sell my car and take the bus.

Although, knowing my luck, the bus driver would probably be just as impatient!

Seriously, is it too much to ask people to drive like responsible adults?

좋아요, 저 진짜 속에 쌓인 말 좀 털어놔야겠어요. 어떤 사람들 운전하는 꼴을 정말 못 참겠어요!

진심으로요. 사람들이 운전대만 잡으면 도대체 어떻게 되는 거죠? 갑자기 도로 위의 다른 사람들도 인간이라는 사실을 잊어버리는 건가요?

저는 제한속도에 맞춰 운전하고 있었어요. 느리게 가는 것도 아니고, 위험하게 가는 것도 아니고, 정확히 제한속도대로요!

그런데 SUV에 타고 계신 폐하께서는 그걸로 만족하지 못하셨나 봐요.

깜빡이 켜는 게 뭐가 그렇게 어려운 거죠? 운전대 바로 옆에 있잖아요!
