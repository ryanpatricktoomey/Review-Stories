# The Reps Standard
Small moments. Meaningful progress.

Calm, tactile, human, fast, forgiving, distinctive, respectful.
No ads, artificial pressure or decorative interruptions.

## Visual tokens
Use reps-design.css on student-facing app shells. Canvas #0f172a, surface #1e293b, raised #334155, text #f8fafc, muted #a7b6cb. Chalk blue #a7cdf8, mint #a8dcca, honey #f1d395, lavender #c9b8ed, rose #e9a0a8, peach #f2b6a2.
Use system fonts with Korean fallback. Page heading 24px, section 18–20px, body 16px, secondary 13–14px. Preserve larger quiz prompts. Cards 16px, controls 12px, dialogs 20px. Spacing 4/8/12/16/24px. Reuse Reps Chalk icons; preserve student emoji and lesson content.
Texture only on selected surfaces, extremely subtle. Maintain contrast, visible keyboard focus and accessible zoom.
## Development
Keep behavior separate from visual tokens. Avoid broad overrides of semantic states. Test mobile and desktop, signed in and signed out, embedded activities, quizzes, archive and Word Bank. Batch related changes; preserve existing lesson files and teacher workflows.
Sound off by default; haptics on where supported (future phase). Respect reduced motion. Every tap should have a purpose.
