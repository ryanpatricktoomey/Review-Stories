문맥에 따라 “발바닥” 또는 “신발 밑창”이라는 뜻이 돼요.

Mina Park had always been a model student. She raised her hand before speaking, finished her homework early, and never even wanted to jaywalk, even when the street was empty.

That was why her family worried her.

Her father, Mr. Park, was loud, lazy, and proud of all the wrong things. He bragged about his high alcohol tolerance at family dinners, even though nobody had asked. He ate cake for breakfast and then blamed his diabetes on “bad luck.” He never listened to doctors, teachers, or traffic lights.

Mina’s older brother, Joon, was somehow worse. He was seventeen and already seemed tired of being useful. He left dirty socks inside cereal boxes. He laughed with his mouth full. He copied homework badly and still acted like a genius. When their mother asked him to clean his room, he said, “Great people are never understood.”

One afternoon, Mina found an old family photo album. Every man on her father’s side had the same heavy eyelids, crooked smile, and strange wide-legged walk. Her father had it. Joon had it. Her grandfather had it.

Then she saw a photo of her great-grandfather at sixteen. He looked exactly like Joon. Mina’s stomach turned cold.

She ran to the mirror and checked her face. Were her eyelids heavier? Was her smile getting crooked? She walked across the room and stared down at her legs. Did she have a bowleg? Was one sole wearing out faster than the other?

At school the next day, she could not focus. Her teacher praised her for being obedient, but Mina only heard a warning. Maybe she was obedient now because the family curse had not started yet. Maybe one day she would wake up, throw a sock into a cereal box, and call herself a misunderstood genius.

After school, Mina asked her mother to take her to an orthopedist. Her mother blinked. “Why?” “I need to know if I’m becoming Dad.”

Her mother drove very quietly. The doctor checked Mina’s legs, watched her walk, and said, “You’re perfectly fine.”

Mina burst into tears anyway. She told him everything: the photos, the walk, the bragging, the socks, the fear that she would have an addictive personality and become just like the Park men.

The doctor smiled gently. “Mina, those traits appear only in the males on your father’s side.” Mina froze. “Only the males?” “Only the males.”

That night, Mina looked at her father and brother arguing over the TV remote. For the first time, she felt peaceful. She was not doomed. She was just related.

미나 박은 늘 모범생이었다. 그녀는 말하기 전에 손을 들었고, 숙제를 일찍 끝냈으며, 길이 텅 비어 있어도 무단횡단하고 싶어 하지 않았다.

그래서 그녀는 자기 가족이 걱정스러웠다.

아버지 박 씨는 시끄럽고 게으른 데다가, 자랑할 일이 아닌 것들을 자랑스럽게 여겼다. 그는 가족 저녁 식사 자리에서 아무도 묻지 않았는데도 자신의 센 주량을 자랑했다. 그는 아침으로 케이크를 먹고 나서 자신의 당뇨병을 “운이 나빠서”라고 탓했다.

미나의 오빠 준은 어쩌면 더 심했다. 더러운 양말을 시리얼 상자 안에 넣어 두었다. 숙제를 형편없이 베껴 놓고도 자신이 천재인 것처럼 굴었다. 엄마가 방을 치우라고 하면 그는 “위대한 사람들은 원래 이해받지 못해요”라고 말했다.

어느 오후, 미나는 낡은 가족 사진 앨범을 발견했다. 아버지 쪽 남자들은 모두 똑같은 무거운 눈꺼풀, 삐뚤어진 미소, 그리고 이상하게 다리를 벌리고 걷는 걸음걸이를 가지고 있었다.

그녀는 거울 앞으로 달려가 얼굴을 확인했다. 눈꺼풀이 더 무거워졌나? 미소가 삐뚤어지고 있나? 그녀는 방을 가로질러 걸어 본 뒤 자기 다리를 내려다보았다. 혹시 O자 다리가 된 걸까? 한쪽 신발 밑창이 다른 쪽보다 더 빨리 닳고 있는 걸까?

방과 후, 미나는 엄마에게 정형외과 의사에게 데려가 달라고 했다. “제가 아빠처럼 되고 있는지 알아야 해요.”

미나는 울음을 터뜨렸다. 그녀는 모든 것을 말했다. 사진들, 걸음걸이, 그리고 자신이 중독에 쉽게 빠지는 성향을 갖게 되어 박 씨 집안 남자들처럼 될지도 모른다는 두려움을.

의사는 부드럽게 웃었다. “미나야, 그런 특징들은 네 아버지 쪽 남자들에게만 나타난단다.”

그날 밤, 미나는 TV 리모컨을 두고 다투는 아버지와 오빠를 바라보았다. 처음으로 그녀는 마음이 평온해졌다. 그녀는 운명이 정해진 게 아니었다. 그냥 친척일 뿐이었다.
