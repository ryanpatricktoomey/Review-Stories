noble

① 고결한, 숭고한 ② 귀족의, 귀족 신분의. 명사로는 '귀족'이라는 뜻도 있어요. a noble cause는 숭고한 대의, a noble family는 귀족 가문을 뜻해요.

like talking to a wall

상대방이 말을 듣지 않거나 반응하지 않아 아무리 말해도 소용없다는 뜻이에요.

hitting on me

연애나 성적인 관심을 가지고 접근하거나 작업을 거는 행동이에요. flirt with someone과 비슷하지만 좀 더 적극적으로 접근하는 느낌이 있어요.

manspreading

주로 대중교통에서 다리를 지나치게 벌리고 앉아 옆 사람의 공간까지 차지하는 행동을 뜻해요.

cut in line

줄을 서서 기다리는 사람들 앞에 끼어드는 행동이에요. 영국 영어에서는 jump the queue라고도 해요.

get it off your chest

마음속에 쌓인 걱정이나 불만을 털어놓아 후련해지는 것을 뜻해요.

Okay, I need to get it off my chest. I'm sick of people acting like they're members of the royal family and the rest of us are their servants!

Seriously, when did ordinary people start behaving like nobles?

Yesterday, I was standing in a crowded subway car, holding onto a pole, when I noticed a man taking up two seats with his ridiculous manspreading.

I asked him politely to move his legs.

He looked at me like I'd just asked him to give up his throne.

Excuse me, Your Majesty! I didn't realize your knees needed their own private kingdom!

And don't get me started on people who cut in line.

This morning, I waited twenty minutes for coffee. Then some woman walked straight to the front.

When I complained, she said, "I'm in a hurry."

Oh, really? Well, congratulations! So is everybody else!

What makes your time more valuable than mine?

My coworker asked, "What was the longest you waited?"

Forty-five minutes! At a restaurant! And you know what happened? Someone tried to push ahead because they had children!

Listen, I understand that parenting is difficult, but having children doesn't automatically make you royalty!

And speaking of entitlement, can we talk about dog owners?

Some people walk their dogs without a leash and act shocked when someone complains.

"Don't worry! He's friendly!"

Wonderful! But I don't want your dog jumping on my clothes!

And then there are people spitting on the sidewalk. Seriously, what century are we living in?

But my absolute favorite is when someone behaves terribly and then acts offended when you say something.

Last week, a stranger kept hitting on me while I was trying to eat lunch. I politely told him I wasn't interested.

He wouldn't stop.

It was like talking to a wall.

Finally, I snapped and told him to leave me alone.

He looked offended!

HE was offended!

My friend later told me I should have been more polite. Apparently, she thinks everyone deserves endless patience.

Well, if she wants to be so understanding, she can do it for me! She can sit there smiling while some stranger ignores every word she says!

I can't stand this attitude anymore.

I try to avoid confrontation. I bite my tongue. I put up with a lot.

But basic respect shouldn't be something we have to beg for!

We don't live in a kingdom. Nobody here is a king, queen, duke, or duchess.

So please, stop acting like the rest of us exist to serve you!
