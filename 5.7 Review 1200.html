특징, 습관, 물건 등이 다음 세대에게 전해질 때 쓰는 표현이에요.

Clara Finch was proud of her brain.

She was clever, quick, and usually witty without trying too hard. Her mother said Clara had real wit. Her teacher called her sharp. Even her brother Owen admitted she was smart, although he added, “for someone who reads books on purpose.”

Then Uncle Martin came to dinner.

Clara noticed that the salt was in a strange location, inside the fruit bowl. She said, “I see the salt is trying to live a healthier life.” Her mother laughed. Uncle Martin stared at her. “Enjoy that brain while it lasts.”

Clara’s smile faded. “What does that mean?” “Your grandfather, your father, and Owen all got slow around your age,” he said. “The Finch brain fog. It gets passed down to the next generation.”

Clara looked at her father. He was trying to open a packet of butter with a spoon. Her brother Owen was worse. He had once asked if clouds were “sky pillows.” He was also completely humorless unless the joke was his own. If people did not laugh, he said they had no sense of humor.

Clara did not want to become Owen. That night, she tested her brain with math problems. She got one wrong and nearly screamed. The next morning, she forgot where she had put her socks. Panic filled her chest. Was this it? Was the fog arriving?

At school, Maya made a mischievous little face behind their teacher’s back. Clara laughed, then stopped. Maybe laughing at the wrong time was another sign.

By Friday, Clara started checking everything. Her handwriting. Her spelling. Her thoughts. Even her hair volume, because Owen’s hair had become huge around the same time his brain seemed to disappear.

After dinner, Clara could not stay quiet. “I know what’s happening,” she told her mother. “Uncle Martin said Grandpa, Dad, and Owen all got slow at my age. I’m next.”

Her mother looked toward the dining room, where Owen was trying to drink yogurt with a fork. Then she said, “Martin tells stories badly. The Finch brain fog only happens to the men.”

Clara stared. “Only the men?” “Only the men.” From the dining room, Owen shouted, “This fork is broken!” Clara looked at her mother. Then she laughed. Her brain was not fading. Her family was just exhausting.

클라라 핀치는 자신의 머리를 자랑스럽게 여겼다. 그녀는 영리하고, 빠르고, 보통 재치 있는 사람이었다. 엄마는 클라라에게 진짜 재치가 있다고 말했다. 선생님은 그녀를 머리가 빠른 아이라고 불렀다.

클라라는 소금이 과일 그릇 안이라는 이상한 위치에 있는 것을 보았다. 마틴 삼촌은 말했다. “네 할아버지, 아버지, 그리고 오언이 모두 네 나이쯤에 느려졌어. 핀치 집안 머리 안개지. 다음 세대에게 물려져.”

오빠 오언은 자기 농담이 아니면 완전히 유머 감각이 없는 사람이었다. 사람들이 웃지 않으면 그는 그들이 유머 감각이 없다고 말했다.

학교에서 마야는 선생님 뒤에서 장난기 있는 작은 표정을 지었다. 금요일이 되자 클라라는 철자, 생각, 심지어 머리 볼륨까지 확인하기 시작했다.

“마틴 삼촌이 할아버지, 아빠, 오언이 모두 제 나이쯤에 느려졌다고 했어요.” 엄마가 말했다. “핀치 집안 머리 안개는 남자들에게만 나타나.”

클라라는 엄마를 바라보았다. “남자들에게만요?” 그녀는 웃었다. 그녀의 머리는 흐려지고 있지 않았다. 그녀의 가족이 그냥 지치게 할 뿐이었다.
