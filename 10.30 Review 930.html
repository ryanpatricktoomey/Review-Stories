1. peek out

문이나 창문 뒤에서 몸을 완전히 드러내지 않고 살짝 내다보는 행동입니다. Peek out into the hallway처럼 사용할 수 있습니다.

2. faint (appearance)

모습이나 형체가 뚜렷하지 않고 희미하게 보이는 상태입니다. A faint figure는 '희미한 형체'라는 뜻입니다.

3. brush past my leg

무언가가 다리를 가볍게 스치면서 지나가는 것을 뜻합니다. Something brushed past my leg처럼 과거형으로 사용할 수 있습니다.

4. so dramatic / overreact

둘 다 상황에 지나치게 반응한다는 뜻이지만, dramatic은 호들갑스러운 태도를, overreact는 실제 상황보다 과도하게 반응하는 행동을 강조합니다.

5. feel someone's presence

누군가를 직접 보거나 듣지 못해도 근처에 사람이 있다는 느낌이나 기척을 느끼는 것을 말합니다.

6. suffocating

실제로 숨쉬기 어려운 상황뿐 아니라 심리적으로 압박감이 심하거나 답답한 상황에도 사용합니다.

The first time Jisoo heard footsteps above her office, she assumed someone was moving furniture.

It was nearly midnight, and she was alone on the twelfth floor.

She often had to work overtime, but she had never heard anyone upstairs before.

The next morning, she mentioned it to her manager.

"That's impossible. We're on the top floor."

"Maybe there's a storage area?"

He showed her the building's layout.

There was no upper floor.

"You're probably just tired," he said.

Jisoo didn't argue.

Three nights later, the footsteps returned.

This time, they stopped directly above her desk.

She moved to the other side of the office.

A few seconds later, the footsteps followed.

Jisoo called the security guard.

"Someone's walking above me."

"Ma'am, there's nobody up there."

"Can you come check?"

"Of course. Just calm down."

While she waited, Jisoo stepped into the hallway.

The footsteps continued overhead.

She walked toward the elevators.

They followed.

Then she stopped.

So did they.

Jisoo looked up.

Three slow knocks came from the ceiling.

She knocked three times against the wall.

Three knocks answered.

Her phone rang.

It was the security guard.

"Don't freak out, but I need you to leave the building."

"Why?"

"I checked the security cameras."

"And?"

"There's someone following you."

Jisoo turned around.

The hallway was empty.

"Where?"

The guard hesitated.

"On the camera, you're walking down the hallway."

"I know."

"But there's a woman walking directly behind you."

Jisoo felt the air turn cold.

"What's she doing?"

"Nothing. She's just following you."

Jisoo ran toward the elevator.

"Is she still there?"

The guard didn't answer.

"Hello?"

"She's stopped."

Jisoo pressed the elevator button repeatedly.

"Where is she?"

The guard's voice became very quiet.

"She's looking up."

"At what?"

"At the camera."

The elevator doors opened.

Jisoo rushed inside and pressed the ground-floor button.

As the doors closed, she heard the guard shout.

"WAIT! DON'T—"

The call disconnected.

Jisoo stared at the number.

A soft bell rang.

The doors opened onto a dark hallway.

At the far end stood a woman.

Her back was turned.

She wore the same clothes as Jisoo.

The woman slowly raised her phone to her ear.

Jisoo's phone rang.

The caller ID showed her own number.

She answered without thinking.

From the other end came her own voice, barely louder than a whisper.

"Don't get out."

Jisoo couldn't move.

"Who is this?"

"Listen carefully. The woman at the end of the hallway isn't the one following you."

Jisoo felt a gentle breath against the back of her neck.

Then the voice on the phone said:

"Whatever you do, don't turn around."
