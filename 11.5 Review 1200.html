get it off your chest

마음속에 쌓아 둔 불만이나 고민을 털어놓아 후련해지는 것. Get it off your chest!는 '속 시원하게 털어놔!'라는 뜻이에요.

defensive

비판이나 지적을 받았을 때 자신을 보호하려고 변명하거나 공격적으로 반응하는 태도예요. get defensive는 '방어적으로 나오다'라는 뜻이에요.

constructive

단순히 비판하는 것이 아니라 문제를 개선하는 데 도움이 되는 것을 뜻해요. constructive criticism은 '건설적인 비판'이에요.

cut me off

운전 중 갑자기 앞으로 끼어들어 진로를 방해했다는 뜻이에요. 대화 중 말을 끊는다는 뜻으로도 사용해요.

weaving through traffic

차선을 계속 바꾸며 다른 차들 사이를 이리저리 빠져나가는 위험한 운전 행동이에요.

face reality

불편하거나 인정하기 싫더라도 현실을 있는 그대로 받아들이고 직시한다는 뜻이에요.

hangry

hungry와 angry를 합친 비격식 표현으로, 배가 고파서 짜증이 나는 상태를 뜻해요.

whine

불평하거나 원하는 것을 얻기 위해 징징거리는 행동이에요. complain보다 유치하거나 귀찮게 느껴지는 뉘앙스가 있어요.

Okay, I need to get it off my chest. You know what really drives me crazy? People who absolutely refuse to admit they're wrong!

Seriously, why is saying "Sorry, my mistake" so difficult?

Let's start with driving.

Yesterday, some idiot cut me off on the highway without using his turn signals. He was weaving through traffic like he was competing in a racing championship!

I honked because I nearly hit him.

And what did he do?

He rolled down his window and started yelling at ME!

Excuse me? YOU almost caused an accident!

But apparently, in his mind, I was the problem.

That's what really frustrates me. People make mistakes, but instead of apologizing, they get defensive!

And it's not just drivers.

At work, I have a coworker who makes mistakes all the time but never takes responsibility.

Last week, he sent the wrong file to a client. When our manager pointed it out, he immediately started blaming the computer.

"The system must have changed the file!"

Really? The computer just decided to replace your presentation with last year's sales report?

Come on! Face reality!

And here's the most frustrating part. Even when you offer constructive feedback, some people act like you're personally attacking them!

You say, "Maybe we should double-check the numbers next time."

And they respond, "Why are you always criticizing me?"

I'm not criticizing your entire existence! I'm talking about a spreadsheet!

And don't get me started on people who whine about everything.

The restaurant is too noisy. The weather is terrible. The coffee is too hot. The coffee is too cold.

Meanwhile, they're sitting there clearing their throat every thirty seconds like they're trying to get everyone's attention!

I know, I know. Maybe I'm being too sensitive.

Maybe I'm just easily irritated.

My wife says I get especially grumpy when I'm hungry. Apparently, I become completely hangry if I skip lunch.

Fine! I'll admit that!

See how easy that was? I admitted I have a problem!

And that's exactly my point!

Nobody is perfect. We all make mistakes. We all have annoying habits.

I try to avoid unnecessary arguments because I know getting angry doesn't solve anything.

But I can't stand people who act like admitting a mistake would destroy their entire reputation!

Sometimes I bite my tongue. Sometimes I just walk away.

But sometimes I finally lose my patience.

So please, the next time someone points out a mistake, don't immediately start making excuses.

Just say, "You're right. My mistake."

That's it!

You won't disappear. The world won't end.

And I promise, everyone around you will appreciate it!

"시스템이 파일을 바꿔 놓은 것 같아요!"

정말요? 컴퓨터가 자기 마음대로 당신의 발표 자료를 작년 매출 보고서로 바꿨다고요?

제발 좀! 현실을 직시하세요!

그리고 가장 답답한 건 뭔지 아세요? 건설적인 피드백을 줘도 어떤 사람들은 마치 인격적으로 공격받은 것처럼 행동한다는 거예요!

"다음에는 숫자를 한 번 더 확인하면 좋겠어요"라고 말하면,

"왜 맨날 저만 비판하세요?"라고 대답해요.

당신 존재 자체를 비판하는 게 아니라고요! 엑셀 파일 얘기하는 거잖아요!

그리고 매사에 징징거리는 사람들 얘기는 꺼내지도 마세요.

식당이 너무 시끄럽다. 날씨가 끔찍하다. 커피가 너무 뜨겁다. 커피가 너무 차갑다.

그러면서 30초마다 목을 가다듬으며 헛기침을 해요. 마치 모든 사람의 관심을 끌려는 것처럼요!

제가 쉽게 짜증을 내는 사람일 수도 있고요.

아내는 제가 배고플 때 특히 심술이 난다고 해요. 점심을 거르면 완전히 배고파서 짜증이 폭발하는 사람이 된대요.

좋아요! 인정할게요!

보세요! 얼마나 쉬워요? 저도 제 문제가 있다는 걸 인정했잖아요!

바로 그게 제 말이에요!

완벽한 사람은 없어요. 우리 모두 실수하고, 짜증 나는 습관도 있어요.
