작은 일에도 빠르고 강하게 화를 내는 성격을 말해요.

Ruby Ellis broke a teacup at her grandmother’s house.

It was an accident. She reached for the sugar, her elbow touched the cup, and it fell to the floor. Ruby’s face became hot with embarrassment.

Her grandmother stared at her. “Oh dear.”

Ruby froze. “What?”

“That red face,” Grandma said. “Your grandfather, your father, and Leo all started getting that same look around your age.”

Ruby touched her cheeks. “What same look?”

“The Ellis look,” Grandma said. “It usually comes before the temper.”

Ruby did not sleep well that night.

The Ellis temper was famous in the family, and not in a good way. Her father had a fiery temper that could turn breakfast into a public event. If the toaster burned his bread, he shouted at it like it had ruined his life on purpose. If he said he would take care of a problem, he usually meant he would complain loudly near it.

Her older brother Leo was worse. He shouted at shoes, doors, soup, and video games. Once, their mother asked him to look after the dog for ten minutes. Leo got angry because the dog “walked too independently.”

Ruby did not want that future.

At school, she tried to stay perfectly calm. When her pencil broke, she smiled at it. When someone stepped on her foot, she said, “That’s okay,” in a voice so tight it scared her friend.

Music class was the worst. Ruby loved singing and secretly wanted to be a vocalist someday. But her father also sang when he was angry, and Leo sang when he wanted everyone to stop enjoying silence.

When the teacher asked Ruby to sing, Ruby whispered, “I can’t.” “Why not?” “I may have inherited a dangerous trait.”

That evening, Ruby told her mother everything. The cup. Grandma’s warning. The red face. The fear that she would turn into Dad and Leo.

Her mother sighed. “Grandma should have explained better. The awful Ellis temper shows up in the men. Your face gets red because you’re sensitive, not because you’re doomed.”

Ruby stared at her. “Only the men?” “Only the men.”

From the kitchen, Leo shouted, “Who made this spoon so small?” Ruby smiled. She still had red cheeks. But she did not have to yell at spoons.

루비 엘리스는 할머니 집에서 찻잔을 깼다.

그것은 사고였다. 설탕을 집으려고 손을 뻗다가 팔꿈치가 찻잔에 닿았고, 찻잔은 바닥으로 떨어졌다. 루비의 얼굴은 창피해서 뜨거워졌다.

할머니가 그녀를 빤히 바라보았다. “어머나.”

“그 빨간 얼굴 말이야.” 할머니가 말했다. “네 할아버지, 아버지, 그리고 레오가 모두 네 나이쯤에 똑같은 표정을 짓기 시작했어.”

“엘리스 집안 표정.” 할머니가 말했다. “보통 성질이 나오기 전에 나타나지.”

엘리스 집안의 성질은 가족 안에서 유명했다. 좋은 의미는 아니었다. 아버지에게는 아침 식사를 공개 행사로 만들어 버릴 수 있는 불같은 성격이 있었다. 어떤 문제를 처리하겠다고 말하면, 보통은 그 문제 근처에서 시끄럽게 불평하겠다는 뜻이었다.

오빠 레오는 더 심했다. 한 번은 엄마가 그에게 10분만 강아지를 돌봐 달라고 했다. 레오는 강아지가 “너무 독립적으로 걷는다”는 이유로 화를 냈다.

음악 시간이 가장 힘들었다. 루비는 노래하는 것을 좋아했고, 언젠가 보컬이 되고 싶다는 꿈도 몰래 가지고 있었다.

선생님이 루비에게 노래를 불러 보라고 했을 때, 루비는 속삭였다. “위험한 특징을 물려받았을지도 몰라요.”

엄마는 한숨을 쉬었다. “끔찍한 엘리스 집안 성질은 남자들에게 나타나는 거야. 네 얼굴이 빨개지는 건 네가 예민해서 그런 거지, 운명이 정해져서 그런 게 아니야.”

부엌에서 레오가 소리쳤다. “누가 이 숟가락을 이렇게 작게 만든 거야?” 루비는 미소 지었다. 그녀에게는 여전히 빨간 뺨이 있었다. 하지만 숟가락에게 소리칠 필요는 없었다.
